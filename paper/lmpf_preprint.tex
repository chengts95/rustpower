\section{Introduction}
Power flow is folklore-solved, yet the literature keeps reporting Newton failures on
``ill-conditioned'' systems and remedies built on the Levenberg--Marquardt (LM) idea.
This paper contributes: (1) an LMPF engine whose assembly cost is eliminated by reusing a
symbolic Jacobian operator; (2) a symmetric-factorization path (QDLDL) that beats
general-purpose LU on the LM normal equations; (3) a controlled numerical re-examination
showing that today's so-called ill-conditioned scenarios yield directly to a dc-started
Newton--Raphson, and that prior performance claims do not reproduce; (4) a repositioning
of LMPF as an instrument for least-squares analysis of power flow equations.

\section{Background and Problem Setup}
\subsection{Power Flow Equations and Newton--Raphson}
Standard mismatch formulation $F(x)=0$ in polar coordinates; reduced Jacobian structure
and the $[\mathrm{PQ}|\mathrm{PV}|\mathrm{slack}]$ ordering used throughout.
\subsection{The Levenberg--Marquardt Family}
One umbrella: $(J^{\top}J+\mu I)\,\delta = -J^{\top}F$. Variants differ only in how $\mu$
is chosen (residual-scaled $\gamma=\|F\|^2$, trust region) and in how many right-hand sides
each factorization serves.
\subsection{DC Initialization}
The dc model as an angle initializer: one linear solve, exact for the angle channel up to
phase-shifter injections. We state precisely what it fixes and what it cannot fix
(voltage magnitudes stay flat).

\section{The LMPF Engine}
\subsection{Symbolic Jacobian Operator Reuse}
The production ACPF symbolic operator (Ybus-driven, pre-sorted CSC) transfers verbatim to
LMPF; sparsity analysis is done once per topology, not per iteration.
\subsection{Direct-Offset Fill}
Diagonal entries and per-column starts are computed from the Ybus base offsets;
assembly is a straight-line pass with no COO round-trip.
\subsection{Normal-Equation Symbolic Analysis and Symmetric Factorization}
The nonzero pattern of $J^{\top}J+\mu I$ is built symbolically; QDLDL factorizes the SPD
system. Measured against KLU on identical iterates.
\subsection{Factorization Reuse (resolve)}
The solver interface exposes a pure-triangular resolve; composite-step methods
(one factorization, several right-hand sides) ride on it.

\section{Evaluation Methodology}
Three confounds have never been controlled simultaneously in this literature;
our protocol controls all three.
\subsection{Dual-Start Protocol}
Every case is run from flat and from dc start under identical tolerances and inputs,
cross-validated bit-for-bit against pandapower ($\Delta V\sim10^{-13}$).
\subsection{Factorization Accounting}
Costs are reported in factorizations (linear solves), not outer iterations;
an outer iteration is not a unit of work.
\subsection{Merit Certificate (Existence)}
The merit $\tfrac12\|F\|^2$ and its gradient at convergence distinguish
``no solution exists'' (stationary, nonzero floor) from ``solution not reached''.
\subsection{Condition-Number Certificate (Near-Singularity)}
$\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}$ of the reduced Jacobian at the solution by power and
inverse-power iteration; ill-conditioning is a measured property, not a label.
\subsection{Fold Location by Continuation}
A Newton corrector chained from the previous solution separates
``beyond the nose (unsolvable)'' from ``outside the basin (reachable)''.

Prior work reported NR divergence and $\kappa=2.3\times10^{12}$ on this flagship case.
With a correct NR it converges in 4 iterations from both starts (both phase-shifter sign

near the fold at $\alpha\approx1.88$, located by continuation.

\subsection{Autopsy: the Tripathy 11-Bus System}
The canonical ill-conditioned system has its fold below nominal load
($\alpha\approx0.643$): no power flow solution exists at the reported operating point.
GN converges from both starts to the same least-squares point
($\tfrac12\|F\|^2=8.917\times10^{-4}$), settling a 40-year literature conflict
(Iwamoto's judgment confirmed; the constant-impedance ``solution'' in a textbook
explained as a different problem).

\subsection{LM Variants under Honest Accounting}
Residual-scaled damping converges parameter-free but pays linear rates in the filtered
subspace; trust region with a sound radius is far ahead (44--48 vs 152--504 factorizations).
We exhibit a case where 15 outer iterations hide 1\,214 factorizations: outer-iteration
reporting is not an acceptable cost metric for this family.

\section{The Role of LMPF}
\subsection{Least-Squares Semantics}
When a solution exists and the start is sane, dc+NR ends the discussion; LMPF's unique
deliverable is the stationary point of $\tfrac12\|F\|^2$ where NR has nothing to say.
\subsection{Infeasibility as Information}
The least-squares point certifies infeasibility numerically and its residual pattern
localizes the conflicting specifications --- actionable information, historically
anticipated by Iwamoto's optimal-multiplier judgment.
\subsection{Re-examining ``Ill-Conditioned Power Flow''}
We call for the term to be re-founded on measurable properties (existence, conditioning,
initialization), with the certificates of Section~4 as the reporting standard.

\section{Conclusion}
Fast instrumentation plus honest accounting turns a folklore problem into a settled one,
